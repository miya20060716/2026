Workshop Paper Submission Guidelines

All submissions to workshops should be written in English with a maximum length of 6 single-spaced, double-column pages using 10pt fonts on 8.5 in x 11 in paper, including all figures, tables, and references, in PDF format. Authors must use the Manuscript Templates for IEEE Conference Proceedings. Reviewing will be single-blind, i.e., authors can keep their names on their submitted paper. Accepted workshop papers will appear in the conference proceedings published by IEEE.

The submission links to all the workshops are available through EDAS: https://edas.info/N35076. Authors can also check details of each workshop and the submission links through the separate workshop websites listed above.
